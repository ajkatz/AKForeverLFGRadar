# Listing text - AKForeverLFGRadar

**Summary (one line):** Pick a dungeon and see how far you are from its door - and how far everyone else running the addon is - with the group finder's listings sorted by proximity and role.

## Description

**LFG Radar** - for WoW: Forever.

Pick a dungeon (`/dr deadmines`, `/dr sfk`, or click the panel) and the panel tells you how far you are from
its door: **inside**, **at the entrance**, **1,240 yd**, **in Westfall** when the map gives no coordinates,
**another continent**.

- **Everyone with the addon sees everyone.** Positions travel over a hidden chat channel; every panel measures
  everyone against its own pick, nearest first, with the other person's pick as a tag when it differs.
  `/dr share off` keeps you to yourself.
- **The door is learned by walking in.** A built-in table knows the old world's entrances to about a percent of
  the map; the first addon user through the door fixes it for everyone.
- **Part of the game's group finder.** The pick follows your listing or the Browse tab's filter, and a pick
  from the panel searches for it at once. Every listing for the dungeon - people
  and groups - comes to the panel with roles, level, class, playstyle and proximity, a group's members counted up,
  sorted nearest first and tank before healer before dps. The panel appears beside the finder window and
  closes with it. The dungeon list is the finder's own, Forever's instances included, an instance's wings
  (SM GY, SM Cath, LBRS...) as options of their own.
- **A right-click invites** anybody on the panel.

## Commands

- `/dr <dungeon>` - pick one by name, tag or a part of the name; `/dr list`, `/dr none`
- `/dr where` - where the client says you are, and how far from the door
- `/dr lfg` - the finder's list for the dungeon, sorted by proximity and role; `/dr refresh` searches it afresh; `/dr dock on|off`
- `/dr share on|off`, `/dr show`, `/dr hide`, `/dr reset`, `/dr diag`
